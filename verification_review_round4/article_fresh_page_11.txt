Chloroquine-era markers
pfcrt was scored as a single unit across the codon 72-76 core rather than variant by variant. Only two haplotypes 
occurred: CVMNK at 0.929 and CVIET at 0.071 (n = 1,873). K76T, the diagnostic substitution carried by CVIET, was 
at 0.071 on its own denominator (n = 1,874), and CVIET ran from 0.125 in the transitional zone and 0.104 in 
deciduous forest to 0.064 in Guinea savanna and 0.054 in coastal savanna. Position 75 shows why the unit 
matters: Asn75Glu requires two base changes within a single codon, so no single-variant annotation names it, 
whereas reading the core as a haplotype resolves it. In pfmdr1, Y184F was the common variant at 0.677 (n = 
1,858), N86Y was at 0.088 (n = 1,887; p = 0.029 among zones) and D1246Y was at 0.011. pfmdr2 I492V was at 
0.549 (n = 1,885).
A geographic cline at pfaat1
pfaat1 S258L was at 0.609 overall (n = 1,896; p = 1.89 x 10^-18 among zones) and ran from 0.824 in coastal 
savanna to 0.544 in Guinea savanna. This is the steepest geographic cline in the marker panel. The same locus is 
the second-ranked gene in the XtX scan, AAT1 (PF3D7_0629500) reaching 24.20, so a candidate-marker 
frequency gradient and a genome-wide differentiation scan conducted on separate matrices converge on one 
gene. F313S was absent: codon 313 was fixed at the reference Phe in all 1,900 isolates.
No artemisinin resistance marker was observed
Twenty-three pfk13 codons were screened, a set covering all 14 WHO-validated and all six candidate artemisinin 
partial-resistance variants, which together occupy 19 distinct codons, plus four codons of potential-tier variants 
(WHO 2025). Twenty-two of the 23 codons were fixed at the reference residue. The exception was codon 458, at 
0.9995: one isolate of the 1,866 called there carried Asn458Asp, which is not the validated allele at that codon, 
WHO validating Asn458Tyr. Per-codon denominators ranged from 1,845 to 1,876. No WHO-screened artemisinin 
resistance allele was observed in the analysis set. Those denominators make this a screening result rather than a 
gap in the panel, subject to one callability qualification: of the 171 panel positions, 46 carry no variant record, so 
the reference base is read there for every isolate regardless of coverage, and no per-isolate depth field is 
consulted. The result is also historical, describing Ghanaian isolates collected between 2009 and 2021.
Within-host complexity
Within-host complexity was summarised on the 4,905-isolate QC set, using the same Fws cut that defines the 
analysis set. The fraction of infections classified as polyclonal (Fws < 0.95) was 0.5972 in Guinea savanna, 0.5387 
in deciduous forest, 0.5000 in the transitional zone and 0.4437 in coastal savanna. The ordering is not monotone 
with latitude: the transitional zone, which lies between the deciduous forest and Guinea savanna zones, falls 
below both.
