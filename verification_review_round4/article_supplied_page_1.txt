Population differentiation and antimalarial resistance-marker frequencies in 
Plasmodium falciparum across the agro-ecological zones of Ghana, 2009-
2021

Abstract
Background Ghana carries a high malaria burden across agro-ecological zones that differ in rainfall, vegetation 
and transmission intensity. Whether these zones hold genetically distinguishable Plasmodium falciparum 
populations, and how antimalarial resistance markers are distributed among them, bears on how national 
genomic surveillance is stratified.
Methods Ghanaian isolates in the MalariaGEN Pf8 release (MalariaGEN 2025) were filtered to well-called 
infections of low within-host complexity (Fws >= 0.95, call rate >= 0.80, clonal pruning), with variant missingness 
recomputed after the sample cut. The analysis set comprised 1,900 isolates at 3,916 SNPs (minor allele 
frequency >= 0.05; mean missingness 2.95%), collected between 2009 and 2021 and assigned to four zones. 
Pairwise Hudson FST was estimated as a ratio of averages with block-jackknife intervals (Hudson et al. 1992), 
alongside UMAP, t-SNE and PCoA ordination (McInnes et al. 2018), ADMIXTURE (Alexander et al. 2009), TreeMix 
(Pickrell and Pritchard 2012), a BayPass XtX scan (Gautier 2015) and Mantel tests of isolation by distance (Mantel 
1967). Fifty-seven codons in nine resistance-associated genes were reconstructed from coding sequence; 
headline spatial results were repeated across six filtering scenarios.
Results Differentiation among zones was very low but statistically detectable: the maximum pairwise Hudson FST 
was 0.00232 (95% CI 0.00128-0.00336, coastal versus Guinea savanna), and five of six intervals excluded zero. 
The maximum remained within 0.00204-0.00255 across the six scenarios (Additional file 1, Table S13). 
Ordination silhouette coefficients were negative for every method. Isolation by distance was not robust, 
reaching significance in only two of six scenarios and four of seven leave-one-region-out replicates. The XtX scan 
returned 19 outliers above a calibrated threshold of 10.840. The pfdhfr IRN triple haplotype stood at 0.771, the 
pfdhps G437 resistance residue at 0.897 and the pfcrt CVIET haplotype at 0.071, while pfaat1 S258L fell from 
0.824 in coastal savanna to 0.544 in Guinea savanna, the steepest geographical cline in the panel. No WHO-
screened pfk13 artemisinin resistance allele was observed: 22 of 23 screened codons were fixed at the reference 
residue and codon 458 stood at 0.9995 (WHO 2025).
Conclusions Ghanaian P. falciparum shows very low, though statistically detectable, differentiation between 
agro-ecological zones at common variants, and the zones do not separate in ordination. Zone is very nearly a 
relabelling of administrative region, sequencing study and collection period, and Guinea savanna alone supplies 
1,334 of 1,900 isolates (70.2%), so geographical and technical contributions cannot be separated; the panel is in 
any case restricted to variants common enough to clear a threshold dominated by that one zone. Zone is 
therefore not a usable stratum for spatial inference in these data. The resistance frequencies are historical, 
describing collections made between 2009 and 2021.
