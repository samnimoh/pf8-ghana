validated allele at that codon, since WHO validates N458Y. No WHO-screened artemisinin resistance allele was 
observed, with per-codon denominators of 1,845 to 1,876.
This is a tested negative rather than an untested absence: the codons were reconstructed from coding sequence 
and read out across isolates collected between 2009 and 2021. Two limits bound what it is worth. It is fixation 
among called genotypes, not among sequenced parasites; where a panel position carries no variant record the 
reference base is read for every isolate regardless of coverage, so the negative rests on a callability assumption, 
and no pfk13 codon has an external per-sample check. And it says nothing about the present. Collection ended 
in 2021, and the pace at which validated alleles have risen elsewhere in Africa means a negative from that 
period is a historical baseline for comparison with current genotyping, not current reassurance.
Limitations
Zone is close to a relabelling of administrative region, sequencing study and collection period. Three of the four 
zones are dominated by a single study, and Guinea savanna, almost entirely Upper East, supplies 1,334 of 1,900 
isolates (70.2%). Holding zone constant does not isolate a technical cause, because study varies together with 
collection year, finer geography, recruitment and host biology; the within-zone tests therefore demonstrate 
study-associated heterogeneity, not a demonstrated sequencing artefact.
The pooled minor-allele-frequency filter imposes an ascertainment floor. With variant missingness capped at 
10%, a retained site has at least 1,710 called haploid alleles, so a variant private to the 25 transitional isolates 
cannot exceed 25/1,710 = 1.46%, and a variant at 40% frequency in coastal savanna reaches only 82/1,710 = 
4.80%. Both fall below the 5% cut. The analysed panel is thus variants common enough to clear a threshold set 
largely by one zone.
The Fws >= 0.95 filter selects infections of low within-host complexity and so changes the target population. It 
does not act evenly across zones: the polyclonal fraction in the QC set was 0.4437 in coastal savanna, 0.5387 in 
deciduous forest, 0.5972 in Guinea savanna and 0.5000 in the transitional zone, and is not monotone with 
latitude.
No neutral mask was defined. Throughout, "neutral" means genome-wide at the retained common variants, not 
at sites shown to be free of selection.
The marker panel depends on a callability assumption. Of the 171 panel positions, 46 carry no variant record, so 
the reference base is read there for every isolate irrespective of coverage; per codon, 21 have a record at all 
three positions, 26 at two and 10 at only one, and no per-isolate depth field is consulted. Related to this, 32 of 
the 57 panel codons, every pfk13 codon among them, have no external per-sample check; for the 25 codons 
where MalariaGEN publishes per-sample genotypes, all 47,012 of 47,012 calls agree (MalariaGEN 2025), but that 
concordance does not extend to the remainder.
Finally, the transitional zone contributes 25 isolates. It accounts for the one FST interval spanning zero and the 
widest intervals in Table 2, and its marker frequencies in Table 3 rest on at most 25 calls.
Conclusions
Among 1,900 Ghanaian P. falciparum isolates genotyped at 3,916 common SNPs, differentiation between agro-
ecological zones is very low but statistically detectable: pairwise Hudson FST reaches 0.00232, and five of six 
block-jackknife confidence intervals exclude zero, the exception being the Guinea savanna-transitional pair, 
which rests on 25 isolates (Table 2). Maximum FST remained within 0.00204-0.00255 across six combinations of 
within-host complexity and minor allele frequency thresholds (Table 4), so the signal is a property of the 
retained common variants rather than of one threshold choice. Ordination recovered no zone structure, every 
silhouette coefficient being negative (Fig. 2).
