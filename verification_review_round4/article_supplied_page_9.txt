The region-level signal is not stable. Mantel significance is reached in only 2 of the 6 threshold scenarios (Table 
4). Leave-one-region-out replication is significant in 4 of 7 replicates - dropping Volta p = 0.0097, Ashanti 0.014, 
Brong Ahafo 0.0398 and Central 0.0427 - and not significant on dropping Eastern (0.1355), Upper East (0.218) or 
Greater Accra (0.2324). Isolation by distance is not robust in these data and is not reported here as a supported 
result.
Population graph
TreeMix was fitted to the four zones with jackknife standard errors (Pickrell and Pritchard 2012). Variance 
explained rises from 0.8970 at m = 0 to 0.9405 at m = 1 and 0.9969 at m = 2, then changes little across m = 3 to 5 
(0.99966, 0.99958, 0.99959). The two-edge graph is presented as a selected descriptive model of the observed 
covariance structure, not as a count of historical migration events. Its two edges have weights 0.152 (SE 0.048, p 
= 7.5 x 10^-4) and 0.805 (SE 0.100, p = 3.3 x 10^-16). The third edge added at m = 3 has weight 0.065 (SE 0.147, p 
= 0.33) and is indistinguishable from zero.
The two selection criteria disagree. Applied across the full m = 0 to 5 sweep, the 0.998 variance rule picks m = 3; 
m = 2 follows only after the range is capped at two edges on identifiability grounds. Four populations cannot 
identify more than about two edges, and that limitation applies to the data available here.
Genome scan for differentiation outliers
The genome scan was run on the 3,916-SNP matrix with the four agro-ecological zones as populations. XtX 
differentiation statistics were calibrated against 100,000 pseudo-observed SNPs, which placed the 0.999 quantile 
at 10.840 (Gautier 2015). Nineteen of the 3,916 SNPs in the matrix exceeded that threshold, 0.49% of the panel 
(Fig. 3). The pseudo-observed set is not matched to the observed minor-allele-frequency spectrum: the 
simulation truncates the simulated ancestral frequency and does not reapply the MAF filter used to assemble 
the analysis matrix, so the threshold is not calibrated against the frequency spectrum of the matrix that was 
scanned.
