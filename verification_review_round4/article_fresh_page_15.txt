A spatial gradient was not supported. The Mantel correlation between genetic and geographic distance was 
significant in only two of six threshold scenarios, and omitting single regions left it significant in four of seven; 
isolation by distance is therefore not reported here as a result.
Resistance-marker frequencies, by contrast, vary geographically. pfaat1 S258L runs from 0.824 in coastal 
savanna to 0.544 in Guinea savanna, and the pfdhfr IRN triple haplotype stands at 0.880 in the 25-isolate 
transitional zone and 0.867 in coastal savanna against 0.750 in Guinea savanna (Table 3). Across 23 pfk13 codons 
covering every WHO-validated and candidate variant, twenty-two are fixed at the reference residue and codon 
458 stands at 0.9995; no WHO-screened artemisinin resistance allele was observed in material collected 
between 2009 and 2021.
For surveillance, these data support treating marker frequencies, rather than population structure, as the 
subnationally informative quantity, within the limits of a panel in which Guinea savanna supplies 1,334 of 1,900 
isolates and zone is very nearly a relabelling of administrative region, sequencing study and collection period.
