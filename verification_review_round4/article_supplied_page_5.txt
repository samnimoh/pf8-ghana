Nineteen SNPs exceeded 10.840. Under the assumed null the expected number exceeding a 0.999 quantile in a 
panel of this size is 3.916, and the ratio 3.916/19 is reported as an expected-null-exceedance ratio rather than as 
a measured false-discovery rate. Benjamini-Hochberg q-values (Benjamini and Hochberg 1995) were computed 
from the XtX p-values as a second, multiplicity-aware ranking. Outlier positions were intersected with the 
PlasmoDB-71 annotation using bedtools, and SNPs carrying ExcessHet or InbreedingCoeff anomalies were 
flagged as lower confidence.
Resistance-marker panel
A panel of 57 codons across nine genes - pfk13, pfcrt, pfdhfr, pfdhps, pfmdr1, pfmdr2, pfaat1, pfcoronin and 
pfap2mu - was genotyped directly. Marker tiers follow the WHO compendium of molecular markers v1.0 of 
December 2025 (WHO 2025), which classifies variants rather than genes and contains no validated or candidate 
lumefantrine marker.
Each codon was reconstructed rather than read from a variant annotation string: the three coding positions 
were located in the 3D7 reference CDS, each reference base was substituted with the isolate's called allele 
wherever a variant record exists there, and the codon was translated. Reconstruction is necessary because two 
classes of marker are invisible to string matching - a resistance residue that is itself the reference residue, as at 
pfdhps 437, where glycine is the 3D7 residue and no record names a G437 mutation, and a residue needing two 
base changes within one codon, as at pfcrt N75E, which no single-variant annotation names.
A mixed call at any of a codon's three positions made that codon uncalled for that isolate, so every denominator 
is the number of isolates called at that codon. Callability is uneven: of the 171 panel positions, 46 carry no 
variant record, so the reference base is read there for every isolate irrespective of coverage, and 21 codons have 
a record at all three positions, 26 at two and 10 at one.
Residue frequencies were computed overall and by zone as the proportion of called isolates carrying the 
resistance-associated residue, with 95% Wilson intervals, which behave correctly at the near-fixed and near-
absent frequencies dominating this panel (Table 3). Heterogeneity across zones was tested per residue with a 
four-zone Pearson chi-square test. For rare residues several expected cell counts fall below five, so those 
particular tests are asymptotically unreliable and are reported as exploratory; no correction was applied across 
the many residues tested, so individual p-values near 0.05 should be read as nominal. Panel genotypes were 
checked against MalariaGEN's published per-sample calls, which cover 25 of the 57 panel codons; 47,012 of 
47,012 overlapping calls agreed. The other 32 codons, every pfk13 codon among them, have no external per-
sample check.
Software
Pf8 was read from the public Zarr store with zarr and scikit-allel. PLINK 1.9 and 2.0 handled format conversion 
and LD pruning, ADMIXTURE 1.3 ancestry estimation, TreeMix with `-se` the population graph, BayPass 2.4 the 
XtX scan, and bedtools intersection against the PlasmoDB-71 annotation. NumPy, SciPy, pandas, scikit-learn and 
matplotlib were used for the remaining computation and the figures.
Full methods detail, the complete set of results, every robustness check that was run, the limitations register and 
a record of the defects found and corrected during the analysis are given in Additional file 1, where all display 
items are numbered Figure S1–S22 and Table S1–S44.
