The analysis set
Of the 33,325 samples and 12,493,205 variants in MalariaGEN Pf8, 4,905 Ghanaian isolates passed release QC 
and could be assigned to an agro-ecological zone (MalariaGEN 2025). Sample filtering on Fws >= 0.95, a call rate 
of at least 0.80, a heterozygosity outlier rule and clonal pruning was applied, and variant missingness was then 
recomputed on the retained samples in a second pass: 1,634,808 variants passed provisionally, 117 high-
missingness samples were dropped, 1,807,331 variants stood at 10% missingness or less on the 1,900 isolates 
retained, and 3,916 of these reached a minor allele frequency of 0.05 or more. The analysis set is therefore 
1,900 isolates genotyped at 3,916 SNPs, with mean variant missingness of 2.95%, assembled from collections 
spanning 2009-2021 (Table 1, Fig. 1).
