effective population size holding drift down. Distinguishing "currently well connected" from "recently separated but 
not yet differentiated" requires demographic inference these analyses have not attempted (§8.2).

7.5 The rebuilt resistance panel
[Observed] The panel in the previous draft was the wrong panel, and this section is a replacement rather 
than a revision. An external critique raised five points about it; all five were upheld, and four were defects in 
code. The panel is now produced by step12_codon_panel.py, which maps each requested codon to its three 
genomic positions through the CDS, collects every variant record at those positions regardless of numalt or 
is_snp, applies each isolate's alleles to the reference codon and translates the result. It reports the full residue 
spectrum including the reference residue. The panel covers 57 codons across nine genes on the 1,900 
analysed isolates, of which 24 are polymorphic and 33 monomorphic; a mixed call makes a codon uncalled 
rather than being resolved to a residue, so the denominator is per codon, 1,834–1,900 called, and is stated 
with every frequency below. Six of the nine genes sit on the plus strand (pfap2mu, pfcoronin, pfcrt, pfdhfr, 
pfdhps, pfmdr1) and three on the minus strand (pfaat1, pfk13, pfmdr2), which is the direction the 
reconstruction has to get right. Every number in this section comes from that run; nothing from the previous 
panel is carried forward.
