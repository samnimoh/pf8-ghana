Antifolate markers
All three components of the antifolate triple were common in pfdhfr: N51I at 0.786 (n = 1,891), C59R at 0.886 (n 
= 1,888) and S108N at 0.908 (n = 1,889), each varying in frequency among zones (p = 2.43 x 10^-4, 4.80 x 10^-5 
and 9.40 x 10^-7, respectively). I164L was absent, codon 164 being fixed at the reference Ile. Read as a 
haplotype on complete cases, the IRN triple stood at 0.771 (n = 1,876; 95% CI 0.751-0.789). Zone estimates were 
0.880 in the transitional zone (n = 25), 0.867 in coastal savanna (n = 203), 0.788 in deciduous forest (n = 330) and 
0.750 in Guinea savanna (n = 1,318). The IRNL quadruple, which adds I164L, occurred in 0 of 1,874 complete 
cases.
In pfdhps, the resistance-associated residue at codon 437 is glycine, and glycine is also the 3D7 reference residue 
at that position. G437 stood at 0.897 (n = 1,892; p = 1.17 x 10^-6 among zones), highest in deciduous forest 
(0.964) and coastal savanna (0.941) and lower in Guinea savanna (0.875) and the transitional zone (0.800). 
Because the resistance residue is the reference residue, the site generates no variant call against 3D7, and 
matching on annotation strings does not recover it; reconstructing the codon from the coding sequence does. 
S436A was at 0.541 and A613S at 0.108, while K540E (0.0095) and A581G (0.0138) were rare. The A437G+K540E 
double haplotype was at 0.0095, matching the frequency of K540E alone. Marker frequencies overall and by 
zone are given in Table 3.
