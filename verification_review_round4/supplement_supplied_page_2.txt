What this document is. This document is the complete analysis record behind the main article: the full 
methods detail, every robustness check that was run, the limitations register, and the record of defects found 
and corrected during the analysis. It is supplied so that any number in the article can be traced to the output 
that produced it, and so that the corrections made along the way are on the record rather than invisible.
Numbering. Display items are numbered sequentially in the order they appear: Figure S1 to Figure S23 and 
Table S1 to Table S52. Every caption carries its own number. In the working report from which this document 
derives, 6 labels had each been used for more than one display item and three carried letter suffixes, so a 
cross-reference could be ambiguous; the numbering here removes that, and each cross-reference was bound 
to the nearest caption carrying its original label. Section numbering is unchanged, so references of the form 
“Section 6.9” still resolve within this document.
References. Section 11 is the reference list. Every entry was checked against a primary source — the 
publisher’s deposit, the journal’s record, the archive record or the issuing organisation’s page — rather than 
against a citation aggregator.
Where to look. Sections 3 and 5 give the methods and analytical decisions in full; Section 6 the complete 
results; Section 8 the robustness checks, including the six-scenario threshold sensitivity analysis; Section 9 the 
limitations register; and Section 12 the appendices.
