Zone sizes are strongly unequal. Guinea savanna contributes 1,334 isolates, deciduous forest 337, coastal 
savanna 204 and the transitional zone 25. Zone is also very nearly a relabelling of administrative region, 
sequencing study and collection period: three of the four zones are dominated by a single study, and Guinea 
savanna, which here is effectively Upper East region, supplies 1,334 of 1,900 isolates (70.2%). Seven 
administrative regions are the finest spatial unit available in the release metadata. Because study membership 
varies together with collection year, finer geography, recruitment and host biology, holding zone constant does 
not isolate a technical cause; the comparisons below can demonstrate study-associated heterogeneity but not a 
sequencing artefact.
The pooled minor allele frequency cut imposes an ascertainment floor set by the largest zone. At 10% variant 
missingness or less, a retained site carries at least 1,710 called haploid alleles. A variant private to the 25 
transitional isolates therefore cannot exceed 25/1,710 = 1.46%, and a variant carried at 40% frequency within 
coastal savanna reaches only 82/1,710 = 4.80%. Both fall below the 5% cut, so the panel consists of variants 
common enough to clear a threshold dominated by one zone. No neutral mask was defined, and "neutral" here 
means genome-wide at the retained common variants rather than at sites shown to be free of selection.
Zones do not separate in ordination
Three ordinations of the published Pf8 genotype-distance submatrix for the 1,900 isolates were compared (Fig. 
2). UMAP achieved a trustworthiness of 0.486 and a Spearman correlation of 0.029 between embedded and 
original distances (McInnes et al. 2018); t-SNE reached 0.507 and 0.115; PCoA reached 0.495 and 0.322. PCoA 
preserves global distance structure best, while all three embeddings discard most of it. Silhouette coefficients 
computed by zone are negative in every case: -0.0156 on the original distances, -0.306 for UMAP, -0.057 for t-
SNE and -0.112 for PCoA. Isolates are on average closer to members of other zones than to members of their 
own, in the original distance space as well as in each embedding. Zone-coloured isolates overlap extensively in 
Fig. 2, and no method recovers zone as a cluster boundary.
Fig. 2 UMAP, t-SNE and PCoA of the 1,900-isolate genotype-distance submatrix, coloured by agro-ecological zone. No zone forms a 
distinct cluster under any of the three methods; all silhouette coefficients are negative.
Differentiation between zones is very low but detectable
