Ancestry inference
ADMIXTURE was run on 3,385 LD-pruned SNPs with 10-fold cross-validation (Alexander et al. 2009). The cross-
validation curve is nearly flat: error falls from 0.84384 at K = 1 to a minimum of 0.83925 at K = 2, rises to 0.83953 
at K = 3, and continues to 0.84488 at K = 8, a total range across K = 1 to 8 of 0.0056. The K = 2 minimum is 
shallow, and the data give little support for any particular number of ancestral components.
At K = 2 the minor component differs between zones - 0.092 in deciduous forest, 0.085 in coastal savanna, 0.053 
in Guinea savanna and 0.042 in the transitional zone (Kruskal-Wallis H = 105.4, p = 1.05 x 10^-22) - but zone 
explains only 1.9% of its variance (eta-squared = 0.019). The difference is highly significant and quantitatively 
slight.
