1. **[P2] Propagate the narrower differentiation interpretation into every live conclusion.** The manuscript’s abstract, p. 9, still says “Near-panmixia is now supported by FST stability.” §3.14, p. 61, still says “the panmixia estimate was always well determined.” §6.5, p. 104, retains a strongest-evidence claim for near-panmixia, and the closing paragraph, p. 177, calls it “the best-evidenced conclusion in the manuscript.” The supplement reproduces these on pp. 10, 62, 105 and 178. These are current conclusions, not quotations of withdrawn history.

   The new §8.1 qualification deserves credit: although its opening still uses “Near-panmixia,” it explicitly distinguishes threshold stability from evidence about the producing processes. The remaining issue is inconsistent propagation. Low differentiation can support a descriptive statement about weak allele-frequency differences across the tested panel; sensitivity to filtering does not identify a mating system or contemporary exchange rate. This is an inference limit, not a claim that these results establish strong subdivision. The distinction is consistent with [Holsinger and Weir’s review of FST interpretation](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrg2611). Carry the new wording into the abstract, summary bullets, answers and closing paragraph; retain the reported numerical estimates.

2. **[P2] Carry the TreeMix model qualification into the concluding answer and confidence table.** §6.7, pp. 109–110, is materially improved. Yet §10.1, p. 165, still begins its answer with “A strictly bifurcating population tree is inadequate,” and Table 10.2, p. 172, assigns “High that a bifurcating tree is inadequate.” The corresponding supplement pages are 166 and 173.

   The answer already qualifies the selected graph as descriptive and does not count its edges as historical events. That qualification should also govern the opening claim and the confidence cell. The original [TreeMix paper, significance-testing section](https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371/journal.pgen.1002967), conditions edge tests on a fixed graph and warns that they do not incorporate errors in graph structure. An edge-weight test in the selected model does not establish unconditional rejection of all bifurcating population histories. State what the fitted covariance model leaves unexplained and the conditions under which adding edges improves its fit.

3. **[P2] The editor’s whitespace fallback bypasses both uniqueness and occurrence selection.** In [apply_edits.py](/Users/samnimoh/Desktop/chromo/tools/apply_edits.py:32), the fallback uses one `re.search` result instead of collecting and validating all matches. With two paragraphs containing `alpha  beta` and `alpha   beta`, the quote `alpha beta` is accepted and edits the first paragraph. With `occurrence: 2`, it still edits the first. Separately, line 31 accepts `occurrence: 2` when only one exact match exists. Flattening at line 14 also joins paragraph boundaries: the quote `onestart` matches the end of one paragraph and the start of the next, altering both.

   Exact duplicate refusal and exact second-occurrence selection passed, so the new guard is effective on that path. Apply the same match-count and occurrence checks to every matching path, including single hits, and preserve paragraph/table-cell boundaries while allowing quotes to span formatting runs. These defects were demonstrated on synthetic XML; this review does not establish that a real manuscript edit used the faulty paths. The README’s explicit partial-write override and documented deferral of edits sharing a run were not treated as defects.

4. **[P2] A missing template figure produces an incomplete article and replaces the previous valid copy.** [build_article.py](/Users/samnimoh/Desktop/chromo/tools/build_article.py:10) verifies the template directory and text inputs but not the individual parts it requires. In a copied template, removing Figure 2’s image led to exit code 0, a CRC-valid archive, and replacement of the previous scratch article. The resulting document relationship `rId10` points to an absent image. The packaging and relationship creation are at lines 188–204; replacement is at line 229.

   This is a semantic validation gap. It does not negate the successful atomic-error tests: write, integrity-check, fsync and replacement exceptions all preserved the original bytes and removed the temporary archive. Validate required images and other referenced package parts before replacement. `testzip()` verifies archive integrity, not document completeness. The published article currently contains the required figure; this finding concerns behavior with an incomplete template.
